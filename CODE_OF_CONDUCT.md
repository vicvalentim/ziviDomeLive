# ziviDomeLive Code of Conduct

ziviDomeLive is an open-source creative-coding and research-software project at
the intersection of art and technology. Participation must sustain a community
in which artists, students, researchers, educators, technicians and developers
can contribute safely, learn publicly and receive fair credit.

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, documentation, examples, events and other spaces represented by
the project or its maintainers.

## Our standards

We expect participants to:

- welcome people at every level of experience and never treat assumed knowledge
  as a condition for participation;
- respect different identities, bodies, backgrounds, disciplines, practices,
  viewpoints and lived experiences;
- communicate with care, listen during disagreement and give or receive
  constructive technical and artistic criticism without personal attack;
- recognize that valuable contributions include code, tests, documentation,
  artworks, design, research, teaching, bug reports, translation, maintenance
  and community support;
- credit sources and collaborators accurately, distinguish contribution from
  authorship and correct omissions transparently;
- protect private, confidential, personal and unpublished information; and
- acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible and help maintain a fair,
  accessible and inclusive environment.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; threats; intimidation; stalking;
sexualized or violent language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention; insults
or discriminatory comments; deliberate misgendering; sustained disruption;
publishing another person's private information without permission; plagiarism;
retaliation against a good-faith report; and any conduct that makes participation
unsafe or degrading.

## Research integrity and AI-assisted work

Research and software contributions must not fabricate, falsify, conceal or
plagiarize evidence. Technical and scholarly claims must remain traceable to
appropriate sources, methods, code, tests or explicitly identified limitations.
Credit and authorship must reflect actual intellectual contribution.

Use of generative AI must be disclosed with the tool and purpose. AI may assist
work, but it cannot be an author, assume responsibility or replace human
understanding and review. A person submitting a contribution must understand,
test and accept responsibility for its final content. Confidential third-party
research material must not be entered into an AI system without authorization.

The project's complete integrity framework and maintainer declaration are in
the [Research Integrity, Human Review and Conduct](docs/en/research-integrity.md)
page and its [Portuguese version](docs/pt/research-integrity.md).

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary, non-sensitive concerns, open a focused
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/vicvalentim/ziviDomeLive/issues). When a public
report could expose personal, confidential or safety-sensitive information,
contact the maintainer through [victorvalentim.com](https://victorvalentim.com/)
instead of publishing that material.

Maintainers will seek confidentiality, due process, proportional action and
protection against retaliation. They may edit or remove content, reject a
contribution, request a correction or apology, issue a warning, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Institutional or legal procedures take
precedence whenever they apply.

## Inspiration and responsibility

This document is independently adapted for ziviDomeLive from the inclusive
community principles of the
[Processing 4 Code of Conduct](https://github.com/processing/processing4/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
and its approach to
[human responsibility for AI-assisted contributions](https://github.com/processing/processing4/blob/main/AI_USAGE_POLICY.md).
It also incorporates research-integrity principles identified in the project's
integrity page. Processing and the cited institutions do not administer or
endorse this project; responsibility for this code and its enforcement remains
with ziviDomeLive.
