# Citation

ziviDomeLive software, its technical documentation, the published SIIMI proceedings article, a future GitBook and a future JOSS paper are **different scholarly/editorial objects**.

## Software

For the software release, use the metadata in `CITATION.cff` as the machine-readable authority.

- software: **ziviDomeLive**
- version: **2.0.0**
- author metadata: recorded in `CITATION.cff`
- software DOI recorded by the project: **10.5281/zenodo.15671506**

Release maintainers must verify the registered Zenodo record before tagging or changing this DOI. Do not mint a different identifier merely to make the documentation look complete.

## MkDocs

This MkDocs site is the official technical documentation for the corresponding software version. It follows the software SemVer and does not currently claim an independent DOI or ISBN.

## Published research article

When referring to the research-creation argument that frames the library as a methodological artifact, cite the published proceedings article:

> Valentim, Victor Hugo Soares. “ziviDomeLive como artefato de pesquisa e experimentação em arte e tecnologia para criação de experiências audiovisuais ao vivo em fulldome.” In *Proceedings of the XIII International Symposium on Innovation in Interactive Media — Paradigmas*, edited by Cleomar Rocha, 615–628. Goiânia: Media Lab / Iberoamérica, PPG ACT, 2026. ISSN 2358-0488. [Full text](https://files.cercomp.ufg.br/weby/up/777/o/ziviDomeLive_como_artefato_de_pesquisa_e_experimentac%CC%A7a%CC%83o_em_arte_e_tecnologia_para_criac%CC%A7a%CC%83o_de_experie%CC%82ncias_audiovisuais_ao_vivo_em_fulldome.pdf).

Cite both the article and the software release when a work depends on the article's conceptual argument and on a particular executable version. The article citation does not replace `CITATION.cff` or the software DOI.

## Discovery record

The [Open Source Science project entry](https://science.ecosyste.ms/projects/36511) is a public discovery/analysis record. It is not a software release identifier, DOI or substitute for the citation above.

## Future GitBook

The planned **ziviDomeLive — User & Developer Guide** is a separate didactic/editorial publication. It has no DOI or ISBN until a real publication/registration process assigns them.

## Future paper

A future JOSS or other additional peer-reviewed paper will have its own editorial citation/DOI if and when published. That identifier must never replace the software DOI in `CITATION.cff`.
