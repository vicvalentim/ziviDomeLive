# Research Integrity, Human Review and Conduct

This declaration governs ziviDomeLive as research software and as a research-creation artifact in art and technology. It complements the technical evidence map; it does not replace institutional ethics review, applicable law, peer review or the assessment required by a funding body or publisher.

!!! note "Commitment, not external certification"
    The references below establish the principles adopted by the project. Their citation does not imply endorsement, certification or supervision of ziviDomeLive by CNPq, UFRB, UFMG, Processing, ALLEA or the World Conferences on Research Integrity.

## Normative and ethical framework

The project adopts the applicable principles of:

- the [CNPq Scientific Activity Integrity Policy — CNPq Ordinance no. 2,664 of 6 March 2026](https://www.in.gov.br/web/dou/-/portaria-cnpq-n-2.664-de-6-de-marco-de-2026-691779232) and the CNPq [research-integrity guidelines](https://www.gov.br/cnpq/pt-br/composicao/comissao-de-integridade/diretrizes), including disclosure of generative AI, full responsibility for final content, traceable stewardship of code and research materials, accurate citation and contribution-based authorship;
- the [Singapore Statement on Research Integrity](https://www.wcrif.org/guidance/singapore-statement), particularly honesty, accountability, professional courtesy, fairness and responsible stewardship;
- the [European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity, 2023 revised edition](https://allea.org/code-of-conduct/), particularly reliability, honesty, respect and accountability; and
- institutional rules and Brazilian or international law that actually apply to a particular activity, jurisdiction or collaboration, including ethics, non-discrimination, accessibility, privacy and data protection, intellectual property and authors' rights.

The international documents above are integrity frameworks, not a claim that every foreign statute applies to this Brazilian project.

## Project commitments

ziviDomeLive commits to:

- prohibit fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, deceptive omission and manipulation of evidence;
- keep claims proportional to source code, tests, executable examples, declared methods and physically recorded qualification;
- preserve versioned code, metadata, protocols and release evidence with enough provenance for verification and correction;
- cite primary sources where possible and distinguish direct evidence, inference, interpretation and incomplete evidence;
- define responsibilities early, credit every relevant contribution and reserve formal authorship for significant intellectual contribution;
- disclose funding, institutional context, conflicts of interest, prior dissemination and material use of generative AI;
- require the appropriate institutional review before any future work involving human participants, animals, personal data or other ethically sensitive material; and
- correct material errors promptly and preserve a transparent record when a correction, retraction or change of conclusion is necessary.

Artistic and practice-based research may include situated, subjective and experimental knowledge. That specificity must be described rather than presented as universal evidence; it does not waive provenance, consent, credit, methodological clarity or the distinction between artistic interpretation and technical measurement.

## Community conduct

The canonical [ziviDomeLive Code of Conduct](https://github.com/vicvalentim/ziviDomeLive/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) is inspired by the [Processing 4 Code of Conduct](https://github.com/processing/processing4/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). It welcomes contributions beyond code, requires respectful and inclusive participation, prohibits harassment and retaliation, protects private information and connects community behavior to research integrity and fair credit.

AI-assisted contributions must identify the tool and purpose. A human submitter must understand, test, review and accept responsibility for every submitted change, consistent with the human-accountability principle in the [Processing 4 AI Usage Policy](https://github.com/processing/processing4/blob/main/AI_USAGE_POLICY.md).
